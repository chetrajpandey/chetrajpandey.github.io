My research explores solar flare prediction using full-disk magnetograms and active-region imagery. These complementary views connect global solar context with the local magnetic structures associated with flaring.

I investigate rare-event prediction, ordinal loss functions, and explanations that connect model outputs to solar physics. My collaborative work also includes heliophysics foundation models, benchmark datasets, and open science for space weather modeling.
