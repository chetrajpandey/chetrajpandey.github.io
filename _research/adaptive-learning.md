Space weather forecasting requires models that can operate on evolving streams of solar and geomagnetic observations. Continual learning offers a way to update these models as new data becomes available.

As a scientific researcher with Frontier Development Lab in 2024, I led the continual learning component of DAGGER++ and supported redevelopment of SHEATH for integration of real-time solar and geomagnetic data. This work contributed to the GEO-CLOAK effort on forecasting ground-level geomagnetic perturbations.
